Cookie notice

Draft · updated 29 August 2026

This page explains which cookies and similar technologies intellectasolutions.it uses. Read it together with the privacy notice.

What we use today

The site is static. The page script (main.js) handles the menu, videos and form submit: it does not set cookies and does not use localStorage.

We do not install profiling, remarketing or analytics cookies (no Google Analytics, pixels or tag manager).

Third-party technical cookies

  • Netlify (hosting and the contact form) may set strictly necessary cookies for security and how the site works.
  • Amazon CloudFront serves the background videos. The CDN may use technical network cookies; it is not our advertising tool.

Those cookies, if present, are needed for the service. We do not ask extra consent for them. There is no cookie banner because we do not offer optional cookies.

If this changes

If we later add measurement or marketing, we will update this page and, where required, ask for consent before any non-essential cookies.
